# RT-01 — Recover live updates after disconnection

Phase: P08 | Order: 29 | Prerequisite: SYNC-04

## Precise task

Publish job updates to the dispatch board with SSE or WebSockets. Include an ordering/cursor strategy, reconnect recovery, and a resnapshot policy when replay history expires.

## Why we are doing this

Real-time transport alone does not guarantee a correct client state.

## What you achieve

A client that converges after missed or duplicate updates.

## What this teaches

- SSE versus WebSockets
- ordering
- cursors
- deduplication
- reconnect
- authentication
- snapshot plus event stream

## Progressive hints

1. Define delivery and ordering guarantees explicitly.
2. Find the gap between fetching a snapshot and subscribing.
3. Treat transport connection and application synchronization as separate states.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Disconnected clients recover missed changes or request a fresh snapshot.
- [ ] Duplicate events do not corrupt state.
- [ ] Updates for other tenants never enter the subscription.
