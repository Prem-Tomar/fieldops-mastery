# Learning and mastery assessment

For every task concept record explanation, pre-run prediction, observed result, test/experiment, and an unseen variation. Link personal evidence in the coverage ledger.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Understanding | Cannot explain | Needs prompts | Explains and predicts independently | Explains edge cases and limits |
| Implementation | Incorrect | Needs assistance | Correct independently | Adapts to changed constraints |
| Debugging | Cannot localize | Needs hints | Diagnoses using evidence | Solves unfamiliar cross-layer failure |
| Tradeoffs | Cannot compare | Repeats advice | Defends alternatives | Identifies evidence that changes the decision |
| Transfer | Cannot apply | Copies recipe | Solves new variation | Generalizes and teaches accurately |

Pass requires every dimension >=2, all criteria passing, and individual evidence for every concept. Mastery additionally requires an unseen variation and explanation of limitations. Correctness/security failures prevent passing.

Ledger states: not-started, learning, assisted, independent, mastered. States may regress after a failed retention check. Independent/mastered entries require evidence and assessor. Shared tests do not automatically pass both people.

Use meaningful behavior/invariant tests, invalid inputs, races, and recovery. Performance evidence includes workload, hardware, runtime/build mode, duration, baseline, and result. Native evidence identifies OS/device.

Weekly: revisit an earlier basic and advanced concept. Final audit requires both ledgers fully independent or mastered. Gaps receive precise remediation with hints and acceptance criteria. Years of experience waive nothing.
