# madhavichauhan1995: independent concept evidence

Cover every concept in [coverage.csv](coverage.csv), including fundamentals. Experience is context, not a waiver. The [learning rules](../../docs/LEARNING_RULES.md) are mandatory. A runnable product demonstration and a short PR/review-session walkthrough are sufficient; the reviewer may use the [optional worksheet](../SUBMISSION_TEMPLATE.md). No learner-authored evidence report is required.

Allowed states: not-started, learning, assisted, independent, mastered. Pair work may be learning or assisted; independent means the contributor can explain and adapt behavior without help. Mastered also requires unseen transfer and understanding the limits. A shared PR does not complete another person's assessment. Never mark a concept mastered merely because its task or issue closed.

From D2, [Question of the Day](../../docs/QUESTION_OF_THE_DAY.md) revisits previously completed work, starting with D1 learning. Preserve the original answer in the review discussion, record corrections and a targeted follow-up, and link relevant evidence in this ledger and the QOTD index. Missing learning answers alone do not block an otherwise correct implementation; the separate mastery gate still needs independent evidence.

The reviewer maintains evidence and scores from actual demonstrations. Do not commit secrets or real customer data. Existing GitHub issues remain untouched by this policy update.
